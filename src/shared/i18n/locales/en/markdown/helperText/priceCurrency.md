# Price

This section manages the **Price** of a Publication.

Edit mode allows the editing of the following field:

- **Price**: floating point number (with two decimals) entry of the price and selection of the currency from the dropdown list, following the [ISO 4217](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_4217) standard naming and abbreviations.

Additional prices in other currencies may be entered by clicking **Add New Price**.
